## What I Did

### Histopathology Laboratory Work

Prepared tissue samples for microscopic examination following a four-step process:

- **Fixation** — tissue soaked in formalin to stop decay and preserve cellular structure
- **Embedding** — fixed tissue placed into paraffin wax blocks for safe handling
- **Cutting (Microtomy)** — blocks sectioned into paper-thin layers using a microtome
- **Staining** — sections mounted on glass slides and stained with H&E dyes for microscopic examination

### Case Study Presentation

At the conclusion of my internship I presented a forensic toxicology case study:

**"Forensic Toxicological Analysis in Cyanide Poisoning: Two Case Reports"**

Covering toxicological screening methods, sample analysis, and cause of death determination.

Various biological samples were collected and analyzed in these cases including femoral blood, urine, stomach content, and hepatic exudate — with sample type depending on case circumstances and time of death.
